About Katherine

Equal parts teacup and trench coat.

Katherine Fletcher writes mysteries for readers who can't decide between a cozy night in and a clever caper — so she writes both. Her Thornbury Village cozies serve murder with a side of scones, while the Dorian Royce Files trade scones for stolen Monets and snappy comebacks.

A lifelong devourer of detective novels and old crime shows, Katherine plots her books on index cards taped to the kitchen wall, much to her family's concern.
