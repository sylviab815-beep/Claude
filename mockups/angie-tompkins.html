About Angie

Where there's a porch swing, sweet tea, and a happy ending.

Angie Tompkins writes the kind of small-town romances you can hand to your mother, your daughter, and your church book club. Her stories celebrate faith, family, and the quiet miracles of ordinary life — with characters who feel like old friends by the final chapter.

When she's not writing, Angie is tending her garden, baking far too many pies, and dreaming up the next love story for the folks of Maple Bay.
