# Amazon KDP Metadata — Cozy Mystery

Produce a separate `[Book Title] - KDP Metadata.docx` with the fields below.

## Title & Subtitle
Cozy-appropriate and clear. Many cozy titles use a pun or a food/craft/animal hook plus a "Mystery" signal. Include the series name and book number if applicable.
**Format:** `Title (Series Name, Book N)` or `Title: A [Subgenre] Cozy Mystery`

## Author Name
Exactly as supplied at setup. Never assume or hard-code.

## Book Description / Blurb (max 4000 characters)
Open with a 1-2 sentence hook that signals subgenre, setting, and tone. Then:
- The sleuth and her world (job, town, what makes her her)
- The body and the central question (off-page death — keep it cozy)
- The stakes for the sleuth and the community
- The cozy promise: warmth, humor, a fair-play puzzle, a satisfying restoration
- Close with comp-style positioning ("Perfect for readers who love [real comp series].")
Keep it warm and inviting — match the tone slider chosen at setup.

## 7 Keywords
Mix subgenre, setting, and reader-intent phrases. Examples to adapt:
- "cozy mystery series"
- "[subgenre] cozy mystery" (e.g., "culinary cozy mystery," "witch cozy mystery")
- "amateur sleuth mystery"
- "small town cozy mystery"
- "clean cozy mystery no gore"
- "[animal] cozy mystery" / "[craft] cozy mystery"
- "paranormal cozy mystery" (paranormal mode)

## 3 BISAC Categories
Choose 3 from the cozy-relevant set, e.g.:
- FICTION / Mystery & Detective / Cozy / General
- FICTION / Mystery & Detective / Cozy / Culinary  (or Crafts, Animals, etc. to match subgenre)
- FICTION / Mystery & Detective / Women Sleuths
- (Paranormal mode) FICTION / Mystery & Detective / Cozy / Paranormal or FICTION / Fantasy / Paranormal/Urban

## Price Point
- **Novella:** $0.99-$2.99
- **Standard / cozy novel:** $3.99-$4.99 (series-entry book often $0.99 or free to drive read-through)

## Series Information
Series name, this book's number, reading order, and the next-book hook.

## 3 Comparable Titles
Name 3 real, currently-selling cozy series in the same subgenre and tone (don't invent comps). State the specific overlap for each (same subgenre / same sleuth archetype / same tone).

## Target Audience
- Category: Adult (cozy skews 35+ but broad)
- Primary demographic and what they're seeking (comfort + a fair puzzle + a town to return to)
- Crossover notes (e.g., paranormal cozy crosses into urban-fantasy-lite readers)

## Cover Image Prompt
A copy-paste-ready prompt for cover generation or a designer brief. Cozy covers are typically **illustrated**, bright, and warm. Include:
- Main visual (the shop/town/setting + a charming focal object; the animal/familiar prominently in pet/paranormal cozies)
- Color palette (warm, inviting, season-appropriate)
- Mood (charming, light, a hint of mischief)
- Typography (friendly serif or hand-lettered title; clear series treatment)
- The exact title, series name, and author name as rendered text, with placement (title top or center, author bottom) — no placeholders.
