# Names & Places Avoidance List

This is the built-in guardrail for the Cozy Mystery Author plugin. It contains a comprehensive list of overused AI-generated names and places that should be avoided when naming characters, settings, and businesses in cozy mystery manuscripts.

## How to Use

1. At the start of every manuscript, cross-reference ALL character names, place names, business names, and pet names against this list.
2. Replace any matches with fresh alternatives.
3. Keep this list as your reference. If you discover new overused names during writing, consider adding them to this file for future projects.

## Why This Exists

AI language models default to the same pool of names, places, and businesses. Readers notice when:

- Every indie romance features a character named "Elara" or "Aria"
- Every small town is called "Willowbrook" or "Riverside"
- Every coffee shop is "The Cozy Cup" or "The Daily Grind"
- Every bookstore is "The Book Nook" or "Moonlit Pages"
- Pet names are always "Bear," "Luna," or "Bella"

This list tracks overused AI-generated defaults so your manuscripts use fresh, unique naming that stands out from the sea of AI-written romances.

## How Names Get Added

During manuscript writing, if you discover you've used a name that feels generic or overused:

1. Add it to the Google Drive document (preferred, so all manuscripts benefit)
2. Log it in this local file
3. Replace it in the current manuscript with a fresh alternative

## Fresh Name Generation Strategy

When you need to replace an overused name:

1. **For character names:** Use name databases (Behind the Name, Nameberry) filtered by ethnicity/origin. Pick something less common. Examples: Margot, Rowan, Jude, Naomi, Kai, Sasha, Ezra, Quinn
2. **For place names:** Think of real towns/neighborhoods you know, or combine real elements. Examples: Ashton Park, Millbrook, Beacon Hill, Copley District
3. **For businesses:** Use specific descriptors (The Broken Spine, Caffeine & Chaos, The Rabbit Hole, Counter Culture, The Honest Brew)
4. **For pet names:** Character-specific. A perfectionist's dog might be "Pixel" (organized, digital). A chaotic character's cat might be "Riot." Connect the name to character.

## Notes

- Use this list as your primary reference for all projects.
- If a character's background is specific (immigrant character, family heritage, etc.), their name should reflect that authenticity, not default to AI generic.
- Avoid trendy romcom character names (too much recent romance market saturation).
- Test names by asking: Would I see this in 5 different indie romances this month? If yes, replace it.

## Cozy Mystery-Specific Overused Names (Avoid)

AI defaults especially hard in cozy mystery. Avoid or freshen these:

**Cozy town names:**
Willowbrook, Maplewood, Cranberry Cove, Mistwood, Pine Hollow, Harmony Cove, Hope Harbor, Holly Springs, Sugar Maple, Cedar Hollow, Misty Harbor, Briar Glen, Whispering Pines

**Cozy sleuth names (female):**
Hazel, Ivy, Willow, Maple, Pepper, Birdie, Posy, Clementine, Goldie, Marigold (overused as "quirky cozy" names)

**Cozy shop/business names:**
The Cozy Cup, The Book Nook, Cup & Quill, The Daily Grind, Sips & Stitches, The Whisk & Whisker, Brew & Bloom, The Purrfect Cup, Hocus Pocus Bakery, The Spellbound Bean

**Familiar / pet names (paranormal mode):**
Salem, Binx, Shadow, Luna, Sabrina, Hocus, Pocus, Merlin, Pumpkin, Onyx

**Cozy cliché method/prop defaults (vary these):**
the poisoned scone with no setup, the cast-iron-skillet bludgeon, the "mysterious tea," the antique letter opener — fine to use, but plant them properly and don't reach for them by reflex.

**Familiar species default to avoid:** the black cat. In paranormal mode, reach for the unexpected — a sardonic crow, a judgmental tortoise, a melodramatic hedgehog, a gossipy parrot, a dignified capybara, a know-it-all octopus, a mischievous ferret.

Generate fresh, specific, regionally-plausible names instead. A real-feeling town has a name with a reason behind it; a real shop has a name its owner would actually choose.
