# Cozy Mystery Editorial Pipeline — 5 Stages

Run these in order after the draft is complete. Each stage has a focus and a checkpoint. Regenerate the .docx after stages that change prose.

---

## Stage 1: AI-ism Revision

**Goal:** remove universal AI writing patterns and cozy-specific crutches without shortening chapters.

**Universal crutches to strip:**
- "A sense of / a wave of / a surge of"
- "Couldn't help but" / "found herself [verb]-ing"
- "Something about [character/place]…"
- "Little did she know" / "if only she'd known"
- "In that moment" / "time seemed to slow"
- Three-beat lists in every description (AI defaults to exactly 3)
- Adverb dialogue tags ("she said softly," "he replied firmly")
- "Additionally / Furthermore / It's worth noting"
- Clinical emotion labeling ("a feeling that could only be described as…")
- Philosophical chapter-ending wrap-ups; one-line dramatic mic-drops
- "Their eyes met across the room" / "a comfortable silence settled between them"
- Starting consecutive paragraphs with the same word

**Cozy-specific crutches to strip (Crutch-Phrase Sweep):** run a case-insensitive count across the manuscript. If any pattern appears more than 3 times, rewrite every instance after the third:
- "a chill ran down her spine" / "her blood ran cold"
- "the air of someone who…" / "the expression of someone who…"
- "a sound that suggested…" / "something about [noun] suggested…"
- "it was clear that…" / "seemed to" / "appeared to" (cap ~5 total each)
- "charming," "quaint," "cozy" used to *tell* the atmosphere
- the town/shop described in the same two adjectives every time

**Structural AI patterns to fix:** every chapter ending on a manufactured cliffhanger; every interview scene following the identical beat order; recap paragraphs opening chapters.

**Checkpoint:** read each chapter for lines that "sound AI." Preserve chapter length and weight — don't compress to "tighten."

---

## Stage 2: Cozy Equilibrium Audit

Re-run all six rules across the full manuscript (Rule 6 only in paranormal mode). For each chapter, verify:

- **Rule 1 (Fair Play):** every clue used in the reveal was planted earlier. Trace the 3+ true-solution clues from plant to payoff. Any clue from thin air → plant it earlier.
- **Rule 2 (Tone Lock):** no on-page violence/gore; warmth present; no bleak chapter endings without a cozy pull-back.
- **Rule 3 (Logic & Stakes):** the sleuth never pockets/withholds evidence; law enforcement is competent-but-constrained; every confrontation has a believable reason + safety net; legal/sentencing plausibility holds.
- **Rule 4 (Suspect Pool):** 3+ viable suspects by the midpoint; killer not obvious before ~60%; each red herring carries a genuine clue; law enforcement chases at least one false lead.
- **Rule 5 (Community):** the shop appears in the majority of chapters with real activity; the best friend gets substantive scenes; a comfort beat at least every 3 chapters; the town reads as a character.
- **Rule 6 (Paranormal, if active):** magic respects its stated rules and never shortcuts the solution; the familiar's info is filtered through animal limits; animals keep their animal nature.

**Arc checks:** no 3+ chapter stretch abandons one pillar; the equilibrium balances; the ending returns fully cozy; series stakes (if any) stay background pressure (≥25% personal-scale, cozy time).

**Checkpoint:** no chapter triggers the same violation twice. Rewrite failures before Stage 3.

---

## Stage 3: Developmental Edit

Assess and fix structure and craft. Cover:

1. **Executive summary** — overall assessment, honest star estimate, top 3 issues
2. **Opening hook** — does the cozy world land and the body intrude on schedule (~Ch 2-3)?
3. **Mystery fairness** — do the clues play fair (Stage 2 confirms); is the reveal earned (3+ clues converge); surprising-but-inevitable?
4. **Pacing** — which chapters stall, which rush; does the mystery sag in the middle?
5. **Character** — is the sleuth specific and midlife-credible (if applicable); is the best friend dimensional; is the familiar consistent (paranormal)?
6. **Dialogue** — natural, distinct, advancing plot/character; talking animals distinct from humans (paranormal)?
7. **Worldbuilding** — town immersive and specific; exposition through action not info-dump; magic rules consistent (paranormal)?
8. **Tone** — cozy warmth maintained even in tension; humor lands
9. **Resolution** — dark moment genuine; aftermath gets a full chapter; ending restores the community and seeds the next book organically (no "little did she know")

Produce ranked Priority Action Items; apply the top 5. Rewrite affected chapters as complete prose.

---

## Stage 4: Beta Read

Calibrate to a dedicated cozy reader:

> "I want you to look at [TITLE] as a beta reader. You read a LOT of cozy mysteries (and paranormal cozies). Give the good, the bad, and suggestions for improving, in order of importance."

The review must include:
1. **Star rating (out of 5)** — honest; most first drafts land 3-3.5.
2. **The Good** — specific scenes, characters, moments. Name chapters. ("The familiar refusing to uncurl during the interrogation in Ch 6" — useful. "The familiar is fun" — useless.)
3. **The Bad** — check, in this order:
   - **Show-vs-tell failures** (first): named emotions, themes stated in dialogue, magic-system exposition blocks, time-skip summaries. Quote the telling lines.
   - **Fair-play failures:** clues from nowhere, an unearned reveal, a killer too obvious or too opaque.
   - **Cozy-tone failures:** too dark/grim, gore, stakes that swallow the community.
   - **Logic failures:** reckless sleuth, idiot cop, implausible legal outcome.
   - Pacing sags, cardboard secondary characters, therapy-speak dialogue, romance-pacing problems.
4. **Suggestions** — ranked list of 3-5 specific, actionable structural fixes.

**Calibration:** judge against OTHER COZY MYSTERIES, not literary fiction or thrillers. Identify the single biggest structural problem.

---

## Stage 5: Targeted Revision

Apply the **top 3 structural fixes** from the beta read.

Rules:
1. Structural rewrites, not line edits.
2. Fair-play and cozy-tone fixes take priority.
3. Verify continuity after changes (names, town geography, clue timeline, romance beat, magic rules).
4. Run a final Anti-7 number sweep and names-avoidance sweep.
5. Regenerate the .docx; confirm no chapter was accidentally truncated.
6. Confirm word count is within the tier's range (expand the shortest chapters with scene detail/community/sensory writing if under target — never summarize).
