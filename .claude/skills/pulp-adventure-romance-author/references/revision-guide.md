# Pulp Adventure Romance Editorial Pipeline — 5 Stages

Execute these stages in order after the full draft is complete. Each stage has a defined goal and a final checkpoint.

## Stage 1: AI-ism Revision

**Goal:** Strip universal AI patterns and pulp-adventure-romance-specific clichés.

### Universal AI Crutches (always remove)

- "A sense of" / "a wave of" / "a surge of"
- "Couldn't help but"
- "Something about [character]"
- "Little did [pronoun] know"
- "In that moment"
- "Time seemed to slow / stop"
- Three-beat lists in every description (AI defaults to exactly three items)
- Adverb-first dialogue tags ("she said softly", "he replied firmly")
- Clinical emotion labeling ("she felt something that could only be described as...")
- Philosophical chapter-ending mic-drops
- "Their eyes met across the room"
- "A comfortable silence settled between them"
- Consecutive paragraphs starting with the same word

### Pulp Adventure Romance-Specific Crutches (strip)

- "Ancient evil," "ancient power," "ancient secret" without specific noun
- "Forbidden city / temple / language / love" without thematic purpose
- "Heart pounding in [pronoun] chest" repeated
- "Adrenaline coursed through [pronoun] veins"
- "A shiver ran down [pronoun] spine"
- Locale descriptions with three generic adjectives ("hot, dusty, ancient")
- "Their eyes met across [exotic location]"
- Generic exotic-place stand-ins ("the bustling market," "the dense jungle")
- "Whispered promises"
- "Couldn't help but notice"
- "[Protagonist] couldn't have prepared for what came next"
- Stock antagonist menace descriptors ("cold smile," "calculating gaze")
- "[Pronoun] hand instinctively went to [weapon]"

### AI Structural Patterns (audit)

- Every chase resolves through one character's specific skill rather than coincidence
- Set-pieces vary in beat type — not every danger is "they almost got caught"
- Every reveal is foreshadowed at least one chapter earlier
- The "they were partners all along" reveal does not lean on amnesia, mistaken identity, or hidden journals as the only mechanism

**Checkpoint:** Read each chapter aloud. Flag every line that reads as AI-generic. Replace with scene-anchored, specific language.

## Stage 2: ARIS Audit

**Goal:** Re-apply the Adventure-Romance Interlock System across every chapter and at the manuscript level.

### Per-Chapter Re-Audit

For every chapter, verify:

- Causal Crossover Tag is present and accurate
- McGuffin Gravity is at least "shadow" (never zero)
- Pulp Beat Type matches the prose rhythm
- If intimacy beat present: active threat is on the page
- Banter-Action Equivalence: both protagonists verbally alive in pressure scenes
- Dual-Currency Escalation: both physical and emotional stakes raised by chapter's end

### Manuscript-Level Audit

- McGuffin Gravity Curve: no two consecutive chapters at zero gravity
- Every act-break raises both currencies
- Black moment hits both currencies in the same scene
- Climax presents a choice between the prize and the love (or refuses the false choice through earned cleverness)
- HEA / HFN pays both currencies

**Checkpoint:** No chapter should pass with ARIS violations. Rewrite any failing chapter before continuing.

## Stage 3: Developmental Edit

**Goal:** Full structural and narrative assessment.

The dev edit report must cover:

1. **Executive Summary** — overall assessment, star rating, top three structural issues
2. **Genre Compliance** — Does this read as Pulp Adventure Romance? Would genre readers accept it?
3. **ARIS Enforcement** — Are all six rules sustained chapter-to-chapter and at the manuscript level?
4. **Pacing** — Chapter-by-chapter pacing assessment. Which chapters stall? Which rush? Which set-pieces over-extend?
5. **Character Arcs** — Do both protagonists grow? Are arcs earned by the events of the book?
6. **Dialogue** — Voices distinct? Banter under threat consistent?
7. **Plot Structure** — Set-piece cadence working? Are turning points earned?
8. **McGuffin Integrity** — Is the McGuffin specific, gravitational, and thematically resonant?
9. **Antagonist Quality** — Is the antagonist genuinely capable? Do they pressure the romance specifically?
10. **Emotional Beats** — Do they land? Are they earned?

Produce a ranked Priority Action Items list. Apply the **top five** fixes.

**Checkpoint:** The manuscript should be tighter and more purposeful after this stage.

## Stage 4: Beta Read Calibration

**Goal:** Align prose and pacing with Pulp Adventure Romance reader expectations.

Calibrate the beta reader voice:

> "I want you to look at [TITLE] as a beta reader. You are a voracious reader of Pulp Adventure Romance — Indiana Jones films, The Mummy, Romancing the Stone, modern adventure-romance bestsellers — and you are giving your opinion as someone who reads a lot of this genre. The good, the bad, and suggestions for improvement in order of importance."

The beta review must include:

1. **Star rating** (out of five) — honest, not generous
2. **The Good** — name specific scenes, dynamics, chapters, and lines that work
3. **The Bad** — specific problems calibrated to genre reader concerns
4. **Suggestions** — ranked list of three to five actionable structural fixes

### Pulp Adventure Romance Reader Expectations

- Both protagonists are competent and capable
- The chase has rhythm; quiet chapters earn their place
- Locales feel specific and researched, not exotic backdrop
- Banter under threat is reliably present
- Romance escalates with the danger
- Antagonist is genuinely formidable
- The McGuffin is meaningful, not decorative
- HEA / HFN feels earned by both the action AND the romance

### Things a Pulp Adventure Romance Reader Hates

- Damsel mode (one protagonist is repeatedly rescued without contributing)
- The magic skill set (one character knows everything)
- Set-pieces resolved by deus-ex-machina (helicopter shows up)
- Romance that abandons the chase
- Cultural caricature of locale or local characters
- McGuffin that becomes meaningless by act three
- Banter that stops in act two
- Antagonist who fails to threaten the romance

**Checkpoint:** The manuscript should feel "right" for its intended reader.

## Stage 5: Targeted Revision

**Goal:** Apply the top three structural fixes from the beta read.

Rules:

1. These are structural rewrites, not line edits
2. Genre compliance fixes always take priority
3. ARIS fixes come second
4. Verify continuity after changes (timeline, McGuffin location, character knowledge)
5. Regenerate the .docx with revised chapters
6. Confirm chapter lengths maintained (no accidental truncation)

**Final Checkpoint:**

- Manuscript is publication-ready
- No AI-isms remain in spot checks
- ARIS satisfied at chapter and manuscript level
- A Pulp Adventure Romance reader would be proud to recommend it
- Final word count within ±5% of target tier
