# Genre Hook Voice Guide

Hooks live under 12 words and stop the scroll. Each genre has its own voice — mimic the voice of the top BookTok creators in that genre, not generic ad copy.

## Pronouns must match the confirmed pairing

Before writing any hook, the pairing has been confirmed (Phase 1.5 in SKILL.md). Apply it religiously:

- **MM** → "He's mine." / "He kept him." / "His enemy. His obsession." — never "she/her"
- **FF** → "She's mine." / "She kept her." — never "he/him"
- **MF** → standard "he/she"
- **RH (1F + multiple M)** → "Three of them. One of me." / "She has them all."
- **MMF / MFF / triad** → "Two men, one woman, no apologies." / explicit count
- **Monster pairings** → match the human's pronoun + name the monster type ("He's part demon. He's still hers.")

A romance hook with the wrong pronouns is the #1 reason customers ask for refunds. Never assume MF when the book is MM, FF, or RH.

The hook templates below use generic he/she — substitute matching pronouns for the confirmed pairing every time.

## Dark Romance / Dark MM

**Voice:** declarative, taboo, possessive. Reader already knows it's wrong.

**Formats that work:**
- "He's my [forbidden relationship]. I don't care."
- "POV: the villain chose you."
- "She said no. He heard 'try harder.'"
- "I was supposed to kill him. Instead I—"
- "He kept me. I let him."
- "Wrong bed. Wrong man. Right obsession."
- "You'll hate him. Then you won't."
- "I made him promise not to fall in love."

**Avoid:** anything wholesome, anything that sounds like YA, "sweet", "butterflies".

## Monster Romance

**Voice:** horny-funny, leans into the non-human. Reader wants to see the tail/fangs/knot acknowledged.

**Formats that work:**
- "He has [number] [body part]. I'm not complaining."
- "Orc, 7'2\", touch-starved. Need I say more?"
- "The mating bond chose wrong. Or did it?"
- "He growls. I purr. We figure it out."
- "Turns out 'monster' is my type."
- "His teeth are the least of my problems."
- "POV: the monster under your bed is shy."

**Avoid:** making the monster human-coded. The weirder, the better.

## Paranormal Romance

**Voice:** dramatic, fated, moody. Stakes are high, magic is real.

**Formats that work:**
- "Fated mates. One problem. I'm human."
- "Vampires don't age. Regret does."
- "She's the last of her bloodline. He's why."
- "The prophecy said 'choose one.' I chose both."
- "Three alphas. One mate. No rules."
- "Immortal. Cursed. Yours."

**Avoid:** sounding like generic urban fantasy. Lean into the romance.

## Romantic Suspense

**Voice:** high-stakes, dangerous, hot. Someone wants her dead and someone wants her saved.

**Formats that work:**
- "The hitman was hired to kill her. He didn't."
- "Witness protection. One bed. One bodyguard."
- "He's the FBI. I'm the target. We're in love."
- "She ran from one killer into another."
- "Trust no one. Especially not him."

## Romcom (Spicy)

**Voice:** chaotic, self-aware, horny-adjacent. Internal monologue energy.

**Formats that work:**
- "I fake-dated my brother's best friend. Help."
- "He's 6'4\". I'm cooked."
- "POV: you forgot you wrote his name on the bathroom wall."
- "One bed. Zero chill. Infinite consequences."
- "My fake husband got real feelings."
- "He said 'enemies to lovers.' I said 'prove it.'"

## Romcom (Clean / Closed-Door)

**Voice:** same chaos, less spice. Heart-flutter, not bed-flutter.

**Formats that work:**
- "Small town. One coffee shop. Zero escape."
- "He's the grumpy one. I'm the sunshine. It's working."
- "I moved back home. He was the worst part. Now he's the best."
- "Fake dating for the wedding. Real feelings by dessert."

## Cozy Mystery

**Voice:** charming, curious, low-key. Murder is the hook but the vibe is warm.

**Formats that work:**
- "A body in the bakery. A cat who knows something."
- "Small town, big secret, one nosy librarian."
- "The victim was my ex. The suspect is my neighbor. Again."
- "Tea. Knitting. Murder. Priorities."
- "She solves mysteries. Her cat judges."

## Psychological Horror / Dark Stays

**Voice:** quiet, uneasy, specific. Don't tell the reader it's scary — make them feel it.

**Formats that work:**
- "The cabin had four locks. On the inside."
- "She booked the room alone. She didn't arrive alone."
- "Check-in was at 3pm. She's still waiting."
- "The host said no guests after dark."
- "Every mirror in the house was covered."
- "The guestbook only had her name."

## Sci-Fi (Non-Romance)

**Voice:** concept-forward, high-idea. The premise IS the hook.

**Formats that work:**
- "In 2087, sleep became illegal."
- "The ship's AI lied. Everyone agreed."
- "Earth had a twin. They wanted it back."
- "She woke up 400 years late."

## Cozy Fantasy / LitRPG

**Voice:** warm, stakes-low, aesthetic-high. Coffee shops in dungeons energy.

**Formats that work:**
- "Retired hero. New job: barista. Unexpected regulars."
- "Level 1 witch opens a bakery. Chaos ensues."
- "The dungeon needed a gardener. She applied."

## Thriller / Suspense

**Voice:** urgent, vice-tight, forward-propulsion.

**Formats that work:**
- "She trusted him. That was her first mistake."
- "Eight hours until the deadline. One witness left."
- "Everyone in the house was lying."
