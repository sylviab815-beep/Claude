# Caption + Hashtag Templates by Platform & Genre

Captions are platform-specific. Don't reuse the TikTok caption on Instagram — algorithm signals are different.

## Per-platform caption shape

### TikTok
- **Length:** 1-2 short lines max. Aggressive front-loading.
- **Format:** strongest hook on line 1 → buy link or "link in bio" → 4-6 hashtags
- **Tone:** punchy, declarative, lowercase okay
- **Emoji:** 1-2 max, used as visual breaks
- **Ban:** long story descriptions, multiple paragraphs

### Instagram Reels
- **Length:** 1-3 short paragraphs. Hook line 1, expand on lines 2-3, CTA, then hashtags.
- **Format:** hook → emotional beat → CTA → 8-15 hashtags (hide them at the end with line breaks or a `.` separator)
- **Tone:** more storytelling than TikTok, but still front-loaded
- **Emoji:** 2-4 used for emotional emphasis

### YouTube Shorts
- **Length:** 1-2 lines + 5-8 keyword-rich hashtags
- **Format:** descriptive hook (helps SEO) → buy link → hashtags
- **Tone:** more searchable, fewer "you'll cry" emotional plays — YouTube favors keywords
- **Emoji:** minimal

### Facebook Reels
- **Length:** 2-3 paragraphs, can lean storytelling
- **Format:** hook → backstory beat → buy link → 3-5 hashtags
- **Tone:** more conversational, longer reads work here
- **Emoji:** 2-4

### Pinterest Idea Pins
- **Length:** 1 short title-like line + keyword description
- **Format:** book-themed title → 1 sentence about the trope → 3-5 hashtags
- **Tone:** descriptive, SEO-leaning, evergreen
- **Emoji:** sparingly

## Caption building blocks

### Hook variants per slideshow
A slideshow has multiple hooks (one per slide). For the caption, pick the SHARPEST hook (the most scroll-stopping one) — usually slide 1 or whichever line tested as the strongest during the approval phase.

### Buy-link strategies
- **Universal Book Link** (geni.us, books2read.com) — best because it routes to the reader's preferred store
- **Amazon direct** — fine if author is exclusive to Amazon/KU
- **"Link in bio" / "link in pinned comment"** — TikTok specifically prefers this; Pinterest doesn't allow links in pin captions

### CTAs that work for indie romance
- "Out now → [link]"
- "KU → [link]"
- "1-click → [link]"
- "Free in Kindle Unlimited"
- "Read it tonight"
- "Available everywhere"
- "Start the series free"

### Avoid
- Long synopses — they kill reach
- Spoilers
- Too many hashtags on TikTok (4-6 is the sweet spot, not 30)
- Generic hashtags like #book #read #author — they do nothing
