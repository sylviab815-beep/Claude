# Scroll Stopper

**Turn any book into a week of scroll-stopping reels — without touching Canva.**

Scroll Stopper is a Claude Code plugin for indie authors. Drop in your manuscript (.docx / .epub / .pdf), and it generates ready-to-post 9:16 slideshows: AI-generated images, genre-matched scroll-stopping hooks baked onto every slide, optional auto-generated music, and a content log so you always know what's been posted where.

Built for BookTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest Idea Pins.

---

## What it does

1. Reads your manuscript and auto-detects the genre
2. Pulls scroll-stopping hooks in your genre's voice (dark romance ≠ cozy mystery)
3. Generates 9:16 cinematic images via fal.ai Flux Pro
4. Overlays each hook with genre-correct fonts, color, and positioning
5. (Optional) Renders to MP4 with Ken Burns zoom + crossfade + auto-music
6. Saves everything to **your** chosen folder (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, local)
7. Logs every slideshow to a CSV so you can track what was posted, when, and where

---

## Quick start

```
/scroll-stopper-setup       # one-time — tells the plugin where to save and how to log
/scan-library               # (optional) auto-detect genres across your whole book folder
/create-slideshow my-book.epub 3 7
/slideshow-to-video "Slideshow-01"
```

That's it. No template hunting. No font fiddling. No "what hook works for cozy mystery."

---

## Three modes

Pick one as your default during setup, override per-slideshow whenever you want:

- **AI** — every slide is a unique cinematic image generated by fal.ai (~$0.05/slide). Most premium look, the most variety.
- **Cover** — every slide uses your book cover with a different scroll-stopping hook overlaid. **$0/slide.** Branded, instant, perfect for cover reveals or when your art already slaps.
- **Hybrid** — slide 1 is your book cover (anchors your branding), slides 2-N are AI (variety + scroll-stop). The best of both.

---

## Music

When you render to MP4, four music options:

1. **Silent** — best for TikTok algorithm; you swap in trending audio inside the app
2. **Bring your own** — point at any mp3/wav/m4a
3. **Auto-generated** — fal.ai writes a custom genre-matched instrumental ($0.04 per book, reused across all that book's videos for sonic consistency)
4. **Royalty-free pull** — recommended sources you can download from

## What you need

- **Claude Code** (claude.com/claude-code) — free
- **fal.ai API key** — pay-as-you-go, ~$1 per 20 slides
- **Python 3.9+** (probably already installed)
- One terminal command: `pip3 install fal-client Pillow python-docx ebooklib pypdf imageio-ffmpeg`

## Output

Everything saves to the destination you configure during setup:

```
<your-output-folder>/
  <Pen Name>/
    <Book Title>/
      Slideshow-01/
        slide_01.jpg ... slide_07.jpg
        Slideshow-01.mp4         (if you ran video render)
        hooks.json
        manifest.txt
      Slideshow-02/
      Slideshow-03/
      music/
        <genre>_bed.mp3
  scroll-stopper-log.csv         (your content tracking log)
```

The CSV log is configurable — choose what columns matter to you (book, pen name, platform, post date, hook, status, etc.).

---

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/scroll-stopper-setup` | One-time config: output path, library folder, default mode, font, music default, CSV format |
| `/scan-library` | Scan your books folder and auto-detect each book's genre + matching cover; confirm in bulk |
| `/create-slideshow [file] [N] [slides]` | Generate slideshows from a manuscript (AI / cover / hybrid mode) |
| `/slideshow-to-video [folder]` | Render a slideshow folder into a 9:16 MP4 with optional music |
| `/upload-video [file or folder]` | Upload MP4s to Dropbox, get Metricool-ready URLs back, auto-logged to your CSV |
